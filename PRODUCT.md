# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Static single file: HTML, CSS, and JS in `index.html`. Fonts from Google Fonts. Pinned by the design spec (Technical constraints).

## Users
Hiring managers, label and agency marketing leads, and PR teams deciding whether to bring Ash Schnoor on for content creator or social media manager work. They arrive from one link (bio, email signature, application) and skim on phone or laptop.

## Product Purpose
A one-link personal portfolio that lands content creator and social media manager work. It shows range across three simultaneous roles without reading like a corporate one-pager. Success: a visitor sees the real work, understands what Ash does, and emails hey@ashie.pics.

## Positioning
She is running social and web for a college radio station while interning on both the label side (digital marketing) and the publicity side of the music business at the same time. The page proves that with the actual feeds and posts, not adjectives.

## Operating Context
- WMSC 90.3FM: social and web director.
- Better Noise Music: digital marketing intern.
- Big Picture Media: PR intern.
- Junior at Montclair State, Social Media & PR and Film & Television.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- One long scroll, no sticky nav.
- Real photos and screenshots only. A block whose asset is missing is left out, never filled with an icon or placeholder.
- Copy in the spec is draft for Ash to rewrite; no placeholder text ships.

## Brand Commitments
- Voice: lowercase, chatty, a little self-teasing, specific. Sounds like a person, not a bio template.
- Contact email: hey@ashie.pics.
- Socials: Instagram and TikTok @underscoreashie; LinkedIn linkedin.com/in/ashie.
- Palette: dark. Ash fits a dark color palette and specifically likes #E954AF; it leads the page (confirmed 2026-09-27, overriding the spec's cream ground and indigo headlines).
- Binding visual direction lives in the design spec PDF (`~/Downloads/Ash Schnoor Portfolio — Design Spec Sheet.pdf`): references are the Pink and Beige Modern Creative Portfolio (Canva) and Sejal Dadlani's Creative Strategist Portfolio (Behance). The earlier black-and-pink editorial direction and the pastel SaaS direction are retired.

## Product Principles
- The work is the star; show it large.
- Leave it out rather than fake it.
- One voice across every section: hers.
